A ferritin result may have different relevance depending on the question. Restless legs in particular should not be equated with general iron-deficiency assessment or diffuse hair loss.

## What is different about RLS?

Restless legs syndrome involves an urge to move the legs, typically at rest and in the evening or night, with improvement through movement. Not every feeling of restlessness in a leg is RLS. A clinician makes the diagnosis.

## Which iron values are considered?

For adults with clinically significant RLS, the AASM names ferritin ≤75 ng/ml or transferrin saturation below 20% as thresholds at which iron treatment may be considered. The treatment route depends on further circumstances. The decision thresholds described in the guideline's Good Practice Statement are based on expert consensus.

A value of 59, for example, may therefore matter for RLS even though it is not below the general threshold of 30. This establishes neither general deficiency nor an automatic indication for an infusion for everyone.

## What does ferritin mean for hair loss?

Iron deficiency may be considered with diffuse hair loss. However, hair can fall out for many reasons. A single number does not determine the cause and suitable treatment. In particular, the RLS recommendations do not establish a universal ferritin target above 100 for every hair problem.

## Two symptoms need two questions

With RLS, we ask about the typical symptom pattern and relevant iron parameters. With hair loss, we consider the course, pattern, medicines, diet and possible other causes. One person may have several causes at once.

## What should you bring?

Record when leg symptoms occur and what improves them. For hair loss, the onset and course help, along with any photographs and test results. Please do not base treatment solely on a number in an online check.

The [iron status check](/en/free-tools/iron-status-check) considers known RLS as a separate context. Other ranges remain distinct from it.
