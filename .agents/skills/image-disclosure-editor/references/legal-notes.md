# Legal notes

Reviewed on 2026-10-25 for the German clinic website.

Article 50(4)–(5) of the EU AI Act requires clear, accessible disclosure of covered deepfake imagery by first exposure.
The Commission's guidance includes fictional people who could plausibly exist. Context and potential deception still affect classification.
For new realistic AI model imagery on clinic pages, the legal review recommends treating disclosure as required.
Alt text, EXIF, C2PA, or an Impressum notice alone does not provide visible disclosure.
No prescribed badge graphic, exact phrase, tool credit, pixel size, or lengthy notice is specified.
The compact design in this skill is an implementation judgment, not a certified legal minimum.

The Commission says Article 50 applies from 2 August 2026. Content generated before that date needs no retroactive label.
The user nevertheless requests model-image transparency across older aesthetic assets.
Do not describe those voluntary retrospective labels as universal legal duties.

An AI badge does not establish that a model is a real patient or that an image shows treatment results.
German misleading-advertising restrictions still apply. A badge cannot correct a false treatment claim.
Do not change clinical content without the review required by the repository rules.
