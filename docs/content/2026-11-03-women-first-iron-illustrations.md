# Women-first iron illustrations

The owner confirmed on November 3, 2026 that iron-related patient imagery must focus on women. The illustration skill now states this rule explicitly for iron deficiency, ferritin, oral iron and iron infusion content.

Four covers were edited with the built-in `image_gen` tool. Each edit used the existing cover as its reference. German and English versions share the replacement image. Neutral still lifes and abstract tool images remain unchanged.

The selected WebP covers are 1368 × 760 pixels. Sharp resized and compressed the generated PNGs at WebP quality 82. Original generated PNGs remain in the task's generated-image directory. New filenames prevent browsers from reusing the old published assets. Existing files remain available for history.

All eight article references use localized alt text and retain `coverImageIsAiGenerated: true`. The image registry records the generation evidence. Article prose, clinical claims, publication dates and medical review dates were not changed.
