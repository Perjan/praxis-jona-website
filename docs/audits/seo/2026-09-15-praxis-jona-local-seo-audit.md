## Verdict

Praxis Jona already owns the nearby Google Maps result for `eiseninfusion berlin`, but its PRP visibility collapses almost immediately outside the storefront. ## Review And Prominence Gap

The latest 20 Google reviews were sampled for Praxis Jona and ORVĒA without retaining reviewer-level data.

| Signal | Praxis Jona | ORVĒA | Gap | Action |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Total Google reviews | 20 | 75 | 55 | Build a steady, ethical review-request workflow for all eligible patients; never gate by sentiment or service |
| Reviews in the last six months | 3 | At least 20 in the sampled recent set | Large recency gap | Ask consistently after care rather than running a one-off burst |
| Owner replies in latest 20 | 0 | 7 | Engagement gap | Reply helpfully to every new review and work through recent unanswered reviews, including critical feedback, without discussing health details |
| Rating | 4.9 | 5.0 | Small | Do not optimize for a perfect score; optimize for honest volume, recency and responsive service |

Google explicitly says complete, accurate information helps local visibility; local results are mainly based on relevance, distance and prominence; and review volume, positive ratings and helpful replies can help a business stand out. It also says categories should be few, specific and representative of what the business **is**, not used as keyword fields. See [Google's local ranking guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091) and [Business Profile representation guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177).
